# Digital Banking Transaction Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes digital banking transaction data to uncover customer behavior patterns, transaction trends, channel preferences, and potential risk indicators. The goal was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support decision-making and improve banking operations.

The analysis was conducted using **Databricks** for data cleaning and exploratory analysis, while **Data Studio** was used to create an interactive dashboard for data visualization and reporting.

---

## Business Objective

The bank seeks to:

- Understand customer transaction behavior
- Identify peak transaction periods
- Analyze transaction trends over time
- Evaluate channel performance
- Understand customer demographics
- Monitor potential risk indicators
- Improve operational efficiency

As a Data Analyst, I was tasked with extracting meaningful insights from historical transaction data and providing recommendations to support business growth and operational decision-making.

| CustomerAge | Customer age |
| CustomerOccupation | Customer occupation |
| TransactionDuration | Duration of transaction |

## Key Insights

### Peak Transaction Activity
Transaction activity peaked around **4 PM**, indicating increased customer engagement during late afternoon hours.

### Weekly Trends
**Monday** recorded the highest transaction volume, suggesting increased financial activity at the beginning of the week.

### Monthly Trends
Transaction frequency increased significantly around the **27th day of the month**, likely due to salary payments and month-end financial activities.

### Transaction Type Distribution
**Debit transactions** accounted for the majority of all transactions compared to credit transactions.

### Channel Utilization
The **Branch Channel** was the most frequently used transaction channel, highlighting continued reliance on in-person banking services.

### Customer Behavior
**Middle-aged customers** completed the highest number of transactions overall.

### Customer Value Analysis
Although middle-aged customers completed more transactions, **young adults generated the highest transaction amounts**, indicating stronger spending power.

### Risk Monitoring
Only **1.5% of transactions** were flagged as high-risk, indicating relatively low levels of suspicious activity.

### Geographic Activity
The highest transaction volumes were recorded in **Fort Worth** and **Las Vegas**.

## Recommendations

### Optimize Peak Hour Operations
Increase system resources and operational support during peak activity periods, particularly around **4 PM**, to maintain service quality.

### Leverage Transaction Peaks
Utilize Monday and month-end transaction spikes for targeted promotions, financial product campaigns, and customer engagement initiatives.

### Promote Digital Banking Adoption
Encourage customers to adopt digital channels while maintaining high service standards within branch operations.

### Develop Targeted Customer Strategies
Create personalized products and marketing campaigns for:
- Middle-aged customers (highest activity)
- Young adults (highest transaction value)

### Strengthen Risk Monitoring
Continue proactive fraud detection and monitoring practices to maintain low levels of suspicious activity.

### Focus on High-Activity Locations
Allocate additional operational and customer support resources to Fort Worth and Las Vegas due to their high transaction volumes.
